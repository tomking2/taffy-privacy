# Taffy privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-16._

Taffy is an independent, unofficial music player for TIDAL for cars running Android Automotive OS. It is not made or endorsed by TIDAL. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

## Summary
- The developer of Taffy does not collect, receive, store or sell any of your data.
- Taffy has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
- Taffy talks only to TIDAL's servers (and TIDAL's content delivery servers) to sign you in and stream your music.

## What stays on your car
- **Sign-in tokens.** After you sign in on TIDAL's own page, TIDAL gives the app access and refresh tokens. They are stored on the car in the app's private storage and are used only to talk to TIDAL. Your TIDAL password is typed into TIDAL's page and is never seen by Taffy.
- **Settings and listening state.** Your Taffy settings, the current play queue and position, and a small cache of browse data (names, covers, track lists from your TIDAL account) are stored on the car so the app works with a weak connection and resumes after the ignition is switched on.
- **Cached artwork and audio buffer.** Cover images and a short audio buffer are kept on the car.

All of this is removed when you log out (tokens), clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

## What is sent over the network
- To **TIDAL** (login.tidal.com, api.tidal.com and related TIDAL and CDN hosts): your sign-in, requests for your library, playlists, search and streams, using your TIDAL account. TIDAL's own privacy policy applies to that data: https://tidal.com/privacy
- **Play reports to TIDAL** (on by default; switch off in Settings > Privacy > "Report plays to TIDAL"): which track you played, when, for how long and at what quality, sent to TIDAL's event service (ec.tidal.com) with your TIDAL account, as the official TIDAL app does, so plays appear in your TIDAL history and count for artists. TIDAL's privacy policy applies. Streaming performance and advertising events are not sent.
- Nowhere else. Taffy does not send data to the developer or to any third party.

## Permissions
- Internet and network state: to reach TIDAL and to notice when the connection is lost or restored.
- Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock: to keep music playing while you drive.

## Children
Taffy is not directed at children. A TIDAL subscription is required.
